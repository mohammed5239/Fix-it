## Project Overview

A unified mobile application designed to connect homeowners with verified, professional craftsmen for various home services. The platform uniquely bridges the gap in the Egyptian market by offering a direct hiring model: users can browse and directly hire a craftsman based on their portfolio, reviews, and previous work (Khamsat style). The app features progressive onboarding for craftsmen, requiring official documentation to ensure trust and high-quality service.
---

## Project Objectives

* Digitize and organize the informal craftsman market in Egypt.
* Provide a secure, trust-based environment by verifying craftsmen's legal and professional documents.
* Design a seamless, unified user experience (UX) that accommodates both clients and service providers within the same app.
* Reduce the time and effort required to find reliable home maintenance services.

---

## Project Scope

* **Unified Application:** One app serving both Clients and Craftsmen with role-switching capabilities.
* **Dual Hiring System:** Direct booking from profiles AND job posting/bidding system.
* **Trust & Verification:** Progressive onboarding requiring ID, skill certificates, and criminal records for craftsmen.
* **Core Features:** Smart search, categories for all home services, detailed rating/review system, and before/after portfolios.
* **Emergency Request:** A GPS-based button for immediate intervention in critical situations.
* **Before & After Portfolio:** Authentic visual proof of work on craftsmen profiles to build client trust.
* **Detailed Reviews:** Comprehensive rating covering punctuality, quality of work, cleanliness, and fair pricing.
* **Inspection Visit:** Fixed nominal fee option for diagnosing unclear problems.
* **App Guarantee:** 14-day warranty period obligating the craftsman to fix recurring issues at no extra cost.
